      <div>
        <p class="dr-section-meta">01 // Operating Definition</p>
        <h2 id="tog-heading" class="dr-section-title">Technology Operations Governance.</h2>
      </div>
      <p class="dr-section-summary">
        Technology Operations Governance is the oversight of IT operational processes, systems, access, service delivery, monitoring, vendors, documentation, and continuity practices to ensure technology services are secure, reliable, accountable, and audit-ready.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="dr-panel-grid" aria-label="Technology Operations Governance categories">
      <div class="dr-panel-card">

        <p>Joiner, mover, leaver logic, RBAC, MFA, privileged access, entitlement review, and evidence of access decisions.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="dr-panel-card">
        <p class="dr-card-meta">Audit + Evidence</p>
        <h3>Operational Proof</h3>
        <p>Tickets, approvals, dashboards, control records, screenshots, logs, review notes, and remediation documentation.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="dr-panel-card">
        <p class="dr-card-meta">Risk + Continuity</p>
        <h3>Business Impact</h3>
        <p>Risk scoring, outage impact, recovery planning, response ownership, and governance language leadership can use.</p>
